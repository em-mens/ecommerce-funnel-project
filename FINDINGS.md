# Findings — E-Commerce Funnel Analysis

## 1. Headline Finding
The view-to-cart stage shows the steepest drop-off — only 31% of 4,268 visitors added to cart,
representing $312,713 of the $379,175 total potential lost revenue (82% of all lost revenue
concentrated at one stage).

## 2. Funnel by Traffic Source
Email converts best at 34% overall conversion despite generating only 10% of total views.
Social converts worst at 7% despite generating 29% of views — a 27 percentage point gap
between the best and worst performing channels.

| Source  | % of Views | Conversion Rate |
|---------|------------|-----------------|
| Email   | 10%        | 34%             |
| Paid Ads| 19%        | 21%             |
| Organic | 41%        | 17%             |
| Social  | 29%        | 7%              |

## 3. Time to Conversion
Users who convert do so within 25 minutes on average, suggesting a short consideration window.
The longest stage is view-to-cart at 11 minutes (44% of the total journey) — reducing friction
here could accelerate purchase decisions.

## 4. Recommendation
Reallocate acquisition spend from social (29% of views, 7% conversion) toward email (10% of
views, 34% conversion). Email users convert at nearly 5x the rate — scaling email volume is
the highest-ROI growth lever available.
